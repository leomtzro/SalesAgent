Takeaway: the poster cold-emails local businesses scraped from Google Maps, not Apollo. Claims 1,100 leads in 21 days for one manufacturing client, and 300 leads a month becoming 8 deals and $40K. I only saw the post text, not the video. All figures are his and unverified.

His points worth copying:
- Local businesses on Google Maps are under-emailed compared with Apollo lists.
- Find the generic inbox (info@) and personal addresses. The owner checks them daily.
- He dropped first-name variables and saw no difference.
- Offer lowered the barrier (faster delivery, $5K minimum vs $10-20K).
- Free mockup built from the prospect's own website.
- AI tags every reply, drafts the answer, sends within 30 seconds.
- The market buys without a call.

## Clinic angle (my adaptation, not a decision)
- List: Google Maps clinics in Tijuana by specialty, enriched with Instagram and WhatsApp number.
- Free "mockup" equivalent: the free audit of how their last inquiries were handled (see [[Offer naming]] and website feedback in Handoff).
- Channel in Mexico is likely WhatsApp and phone before email. Test, do not assume.
- Reply speed under a minute is our own product promise, so the outreach itself should demonstrate it.
- Constraints: Mexican privacy law (LFPDPPP) on contacting people, WhatsApp business messaging rules, no health claims in outreach. Confirm with a lawyer before volume.
