The five most common excuses, sorted by where the person places the blame. The excuse tells you how deep the real issue is. Never get stuck on what happens at the surface.

## Where people cast their power (distortions of reality)
People blame things for their current state in this order:
1. **Circumstances (surface):** "I must get what I want when I want it, and must not get what I don't want. If I don't, I can't stand it."
2. **Others (deep):** "People must treat me fairly and kindly; if they don't, they're no good and deserve to be condemned and punished."
3. **Self (core):** "I must do well, or else I'm no good."

## Circumstances (surface)

### 1. Time: "Not a good time right now"
- **Micro (hour by hour).** Core response: you have time, you're just doing things that make you feel you don't.
  - "I used to have the same problem, I get it."
  - "What we're going to do is cut out 90% of what makes you feel overwhelmed about doing this one thing."
- **Macro (seasonal): "Now isn't a good time."** Core response: you'll always be busy, so get the most help when you're busy, so it lasts and gets results.
  - "Totally understand."
  - "Good news: the busier you are, the better the time to start. Life always has busy seasons, and if you can't do it while busy, it won't last. You want it to last, right? Then what better time?"
  - "And if you're busy now, what better time to have all the support than when you need it most?"
- **When/then fallacy: "When I have time, I'll start."** Core response: make them see the fallacy. "I understand." Then walk them through it: "When I get better, I'll go to the hospital."

### 2. Value: "Can't afford it / too expensive"
- **Why a lot is good:** it's exactly because this is a lot of money to you that you'll succeed: you'll be committed.
- **Why it's not a lot:** is it worth it to reach the dream outcome? Frame: there's no degree for getting leads; I can get them for a fraction of what you stand to make.
- **What money is for:** a resource to maximize time, the scarcest resource. With money you buy knowledge.
- **Why you don't need money:** resourcefulness, not resources. "If someone in a similar or worse situation can, why can't you?"

### 3. Fit: "Not sure if it's for me" (the special snowflake)
- **New identity, new priorities:** we vote with our wallet and our time for what we care about.
- **Pain of change:** you have to change to change. Is the pain of staying the same greater than the pain of change? Are you in enough pain?
- **Hypothetical:** "If this were perfect, would you do it?" After yes, ask about the difference between perfect and what we've got.
  - If "no idea": ask if something else is going on.
  - If they name something: compromise on it.

## Others (deep)

### 4. Authority: "I have to talk to someone with more power than me"
- **Isolate and collapse the foil:** "What do you think the person with authority wouldn't like?"
- **They already gave permission:** "Do they approve of your current struggle? Are they happy you're struggling?" If no: why would they disapprove of something that fixes what they already disapprove of?
- **Role reversal:** "If the roles were reversed, would you support this decision?"
- **Permission vs. support:** you're asking for permission, not support. The realization: you never needed permission, just support.
- Prevent it before the call: see "kill zombies upfront" in [[Sales call rules]].

## Self (core)

### 5. Avoidance: "I need to think about it"
**Past: "This is happening so fast."**
- This isn't a fast decision; you've been choosing not to fix this for a long time. Now you can change that.
- Ask: "What are you most afraid of happening if you buy?"
- Don't let it burn you twice: a past bad investment shouldn't stop a good one. Don't let a past mistake control your present and block a better future.
- "Do you think you're in this position because you've struggled to decide in the past? Have you been in this conversation and said no before?"

**Present: they don't know how to make a decision.**
- Rocking chair: instead of going home to sit in a rocking chair and "think about it," let's do it now. Deciding doesn't take time, it takes information. Let's talk, and I'll give you the information.
- Decide with them, in order:
  1. Belief: do you believe this will help you achieve what you want?
  2. Trust: do you trust me to keep my word?
  3. Fit: do you think it'll work for you?
  4. Money: do you have it? Help them if not.
- Include inside information, i.e. a free trial.

**Future: deciding is choosing which future you want to live.** If the service brings them closer to their dreams, the choice is easy.
- "What do 5 more years of the same look like?"
- Outcome classifying:
  1. You do it and get results: risk-free, and life is awesome.
  2. You don't do it and don't get results: risk-free, but it sucks.
  3. You do it and don't get results, but there's a guarantee: still risk-free.
- "You don't want to struggle forever, right? If you'll fix it eventually, do it now and enjoy the results sooner."
- "Does making this decision get you closer or further from your goal? Keep making decisions that get you closer and you'll get there."
