The routine for building and duplicating sales skill: Hormozi's third framework, "how to duplicate this process across salespeople in any niche in 7 days or less."

## Schedule: 60 minutes a day, 5 days a week
| Block | Minutes | What |
|---|---|---|
| Script | 25 | Read the script out loud ([[C.L.O.S.E.R]]) |
| Obstacles | 5 | Obstacle drill ([[Objection drills]]) |
| Recording | 30 | Review a recorded call |

## Review questions for every recording
1. What went right?
2. What went wrong?
3. What are we going to do next time?

## Alternative split (from the 6 C's section of the notes)
- How to listen (interventions should be surgical, not waffle): 30 minutes.
- How to talk (practice tonality, [[Tonality drills]]): 25 minutes.
- Overcoming obstacles: 5 minutes.

The two splits differ; Leo hasn't chosen yet (open question #3 in [[Open questions - Hormozi notes]]). Default to the first until decided.

## For the agent
- Run the review questions on every call transcript Leo provides and save the answers next to the call notes.
- Track patterns across reviews (repeated objections, where calls stall) and report them weekly.
