Every sales conversation must end with the other person giving up something they value (time, reputation or money) and the deal moving to a next step. "Se ve muy bien, avísame cuando esté listo" is not a result.

## The ladder (proposed, untested)
1. **Time:** a booked next call with a date and hour.
2. **Information:** they share something real, such as a screenshot of last week's unanswered WhatsApp chats, or their monthly appointment and no-show numbers.
3. **Access:** read access to their WhatsApp Business or calendar for the pilot.
4. **Introduction:** a meeting with the partner, administrator or doctor who decides.
5. **Money:** a low-fee 2-week paid pilot, then a deposit or first month.

Ask for one rung above what feels comfortable. If they refuse the small ask (rung 2), mark the lead cold: they do not feel the problem enough.

## Rules
- Decide the commitment you want before the call.
- A refused ask is useful data; write down what they said.
- Match it with [[Ask for the smallest first yes]]: start at a rung they will accept, then chain upward.
- The owner's own definition of the win goes into the pilot: [[Pilot success criteria from the owner's answer]].
