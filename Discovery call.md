First call is discovery; second call is the close. Discovery gives you the ammunition you use on the closing call. A weak discovery is a great way to make sure objections blow up in your face.

## Real discovery questions
1. **Intention:** why are they buying this?
2. **Change:** what changes as a result?
3. **Expectations:** how will your life look different?
4. **Stakeholders:** who else in your life is affected by this?

## Common error
Over-educating on the product and under-educating yourself on the prospect.

## Qualify, don't just quote
Make sure they have the money to spend and the problem to solve before giving the price.
