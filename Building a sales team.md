## The 6 C's to grow and train teams
1. **C.L.O.S.E.R sequence:** a question-based framework that follows the script ([[C.L.O.S.E.R]]).
2. **Consistent daily training and conviction** ([[Daily sales training]], [[Conviction framework]]).
3. **Call recordings.**
4. **Communication cycles.**
5. **Cuts.**
6. **Competition.**

Items 4–6 aren't explained in the notes: open questions #5–7 in [[Open questions - Hormozi notes]].

## Hiring a sales director
Hire one person who can hire a lot of other people.
- **Who:** someone who has been there and done that; judged by the quality of the metrics they track (do they connect metrics to revenue?) and their demeanor.
- **How to find them:** outreach to strangers, or a recruiter.

## Scaling to 40 reps (Hormozi's example)
- 5–6 recruiters, plus content and outbound to attract reps.
- An internal training course: game tape, how to sell this product, role-play.

## Compensation
- What reps get: money (compensation for setters and closers; structure is open question #8) and non-money rewards (recognition, praise, attention, approval, perks, status).
- Under 10% of revenue going to the sales team is flagged as a possible pain point.
- Questions to answer before setting pay: what skills do I need, and how rare are they?
