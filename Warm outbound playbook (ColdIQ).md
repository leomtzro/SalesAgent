Takeaway: reach people who already know you, scored into tiers, with a two-layer message. Author says it made his agency $7M a year. He sells ColdIQ and Instantly, so tool picks are partly marketing. Numbers are his.

**Flow:** content brings people in, find 4 warm audiences (post engagers, website visitors, newsletter subscribers, signups), pull them into Claude Code, score and tier, write the 2-layer message, set up sending, review every email before launch.

**Tiers, each with its own effort:**
- Tier 1 fully by hand (emails, DMs, calls, sometimes a gift)
- Tier 2 semi-automated, split into micro-lists that share one reason to buy
- Tier 3 fully automated

**2-layer message:** Layer 1 is how they found you (the post, the date they subscribed). Layer 2 is what is happening at their company now (hiring, new role). Layer 1 shows interest, layer 2 shows budget moving.

**Sending rules:** secondary domains and mailboxes so the main domain stays safe, at least 2 weeks of warm-up before real sends, drafts only in the sequencer so a human reads every email before launch, send signup follow-ups within a few weeks (his waited 88-93 days).

**Caution:** LinkedIn's terms ban automation. He does not automate his own.

## Clinic angle
- Our warm audiences are people who messaged a clinic, followed its Instagram, or left a form. Same logic, WhatsApp instead of email.
- Reactivation of old patients = his "signups" campaign. Matches our reactivation offer.
- Draft-then-human-review fits our rule: patient agent hands off to a human.
