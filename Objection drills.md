How to practice handling objections. The responses themselves live in [[The 5 excuses]]; this note is the practice method.

## The drill
1. Pick one excuse from [[The 5 excuses]] (time, value, fit, authority, avoidance).
2. The agent plays the prospect and raises it; Leo responds out loud using questions, restatements and a story ([[Sales call rules]]).
3. Repeat until the response is natural, then switch excuses.

## Stories that break objections
Have a short story or metaphor ready for each common objection. Example from the notes:
- Prospect: "It's a busy season."
- Response: "Someone had the same issue. The difference is she paid for it in time. She waited, things stayed the same, not bad but not as good as they could be, and eventually she bought. The only difference is that it was 6 months later."

## Rules
- Role-play the common objections as part of [[Daily sales training]]; this is also how to raise [[Close rate]].
- Practice on unqualified leads too: free practice with virtually no downside ([[Salesperson mindset]]).
- Every new objection heard on a real call gets added here with the best answer found.
