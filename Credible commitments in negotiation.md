Takeaway: a promise or limit only changes the other side's behavior if it is believable. We make ours believable by limiting our own options on purpose.

## The moves
1. Make the claim binding. Fixed monthly price and a round guarantee that costs us something if we miss.
2. Limited authority. "The price is set by our delivery cost; I cannot go below it." True only if it is true.
3. Bundle issues. Trade across issues instead of cutting price.
4. Face-saving exit. Let the owner change position without saying they were wrong.
5. Give a clear stopping point. A concession needs a round line (one extra bonus, then done), or the other side keeps pushing.
6. Put reputation at stake only where we are in many conversations at once. In one conversation it carries no weight.
7. Make the other side move first when we cannot credibly threaten. For us: the owner commits to the baseline numbers before we start.

## Clinic scripts (drafts, Spanish to be reviewed)
- Binding guarantee: "Un precio fijo al mes. Si en 60 días no agendamos al menos [número por definir] pacientes nuevos, el mes dos va por nuestra cuenta." English: "One fixed price a month. If in 60 days we do not book at least [number to be set] new patients, month two is on us." The number is `unknown`; Leo decides ([[Guarantees]]).
- Discount request: "El precio lo define el costo de cada agente y no puedo bajarlo. Lo que sí puedo es incluir la reactivación de su lista de pacientes sin costo si firmamos seis meses." English: "The price is set by each agent's cost and I cannot lower it. What I can do is include reactivating your patient list at no extra cost if we sign for six months."
- Face-saving exit: "Esas agencias vendían clics; nosotros cobramos por cita agendada, es otro trato." English: "Those agencies sold clicks; we get paid per booked appointment, a different deal."

## Rules
- Never bluff a limit. If a limit is false and gets called, every later limit loses weight.
- Pair with [[Label mirror silence objection method]] and [[Label the pressure behind a price objection]] for what to say before using a limit.
- Offer structure lives in [[Grand Slam offer]] and [[Guarantees]]; this note is how we hold the line in the conversation.
- The patient-facing agent gets limited authority too: it offers only published prices and slots. See [[Front desk and back office agent split]].

## Why it holds elsewhere (shared rule)
Removing your own escape route makes your word believable. Ulysses tied to the mast, a manager who truly cannot approve above 10 percent, a union negotiator who must go to a member vote: someone who cannot give in is harder to push.
