Takeaway: the cheapest, most reliable way to find new customers. Contacts are leads. Reach out daily, give value, ask for friends not sales.

## The 11 steps
1. Get a list: contacts = leads.
2. Pick a platform: ideally where you have the most contacts (X, IG, TikTok, YouTube, etc).
3. Personalize: spend 30 seconds looking at what is new in their life and ask about it.
4. Reach out to 100 people a day. Reach out 3 times, like you want to get hold of them.
5. What to say, A.C.A.: Acknowledge, Compliment, Ask a next question that leads the conversation toward your offer.
6. Invite friends: "By the way, do you know anybody who (their struggle), looking to (dream outcome) in (time delay)? I'm taking on 5 case studies for free, that's all I can handle right now. I want testimonials. I help them (dream outcome) without (effort and sacrifice). I even guarantee it or I work with them until they do. I just had X work with me on it, even though she (struggle)... does anyone come to mind?" If no: no worries.
   - Do not ask them to buy. Do not ask if they know anyone (you need a friend in the answer).
7. Easiest offer: "Since I'm only taking 5 people, I'll give you all the attention you need, as long as you promise to use it, give me feedback and leave a killer review."
   - Why free: get the reps in, you probably are not good yet, learn by doing, free value makes positive testimonials likelier, you get feedback. Thank them right away for feedback, take it as a curious cat.
   - Free customers make money 3 ways: they convert to paying, they refer paying customers, their testimonials bring paying customers.
   - Hinge method: ask the friend's name, send a picture with your client to the friend to start the conversation.
8. Make it easy to say yes to. Yes = paying customer. No = information: "what would I have to do to make it worthwhile for you to continue?" Exposes hidden costs (hidden costs = inverse of value).
9. Restart at the top and go through the list again. Kill the ego.
10. Start charging: swap free for X% off for the next 5, then less, until you get 5 full-price customers. Every 5 customers, charge 20% more. Prepay + guarantee: "Would you rather get less today (payment plan) or get all your money back (guarantee)?" Paying up front unlocks the guarantee.
11. Keep the list warm: after consistent value, use the magical 9 words: "Are you still looking to (4-word dream outcome)?"

## Raising perceived likelihood of achievement
Show you have done what you offer. Show you helped people just like them. Show volume of happy customers. Certifications and third-party accreditations. Numbers, stats, research. Experts vouching (brand borrowing). Something new they have not failed with before. Celebrity endorsement. A guarantee (skin in the game). Describe their current pain very specifically. If possible demonstrate the outcome live (agency example: play a call from a client's lead and ask "can you handle a call like this? how many?").
