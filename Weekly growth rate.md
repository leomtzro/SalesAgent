Takeaway: judge the early business by how fast one number grows each week, not by its size.

## Definition
Weekly growth rate = (this week's count minus last week's count) ÷ last week's count.

## Which count
Proposed: consultations booked through our system, summed across all clients. Alternative: active paying clinics. Leo to choose. Both are `unknown` today (no clients yet).

## Why it matters (Graham's arithmetic)
Starting at 100 and growing 10 percent a week gives about 14,000 after a year and about 2 million after two. The small start is the beginning of the curve, not proof it is too small.

## Benchmark
Graham gives 10 percent a week as an example, not a standard. For our market: `unknown`. Do not use it as a target until Leo approves.

## How to fix a low rate
Read it with [[Sales funnel metrics]] and fix the earliest weak stage first: contacts, replies, calls booked ([[Show rate]]), then [[Close rate]]. Also check whether the hand-done steps in [[Recruit the first clients by hand]] are being skipped.

## Limits
A rate on tiny numbers swings wildly (1 to 2 is 100 percent). Look at several weeks together.
