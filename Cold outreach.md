Takeaway: advertise to strangers in private (email, phone, SMS, DM). If you can contact someone one-to-one, you can cold outreach. Strangers present 3 problems: no way to contact them, they ignore us, they are not interested.

## Build a list (3 ways)
1. Scrape 'em (easiest): outbound or lead-scraping software. Subscribe to as many as you can, search by your criteria, test a sample of a few hundred from each, if the info is current and people respond and match what the software claims, buy more.
2. Buy 'em (mid): brokers make a list to your criteria, send a sample, test each, stick with the ones that perform.
3. Look for 'em (hardest, costs only time): join groups where your audience lives, when someone fits check for a way to contact them (directory), add them, otherwise reach out inside the platform.
- Interest groups are the warmest cold audience: problem aware, looking for a solution, engaged.
- Leo's action: search "outbound leads scraping tool" or "database lead scraping", find brokers. With more time than money start with elbow grease.

## What to say
- Cold should look like warm. Personalize: area code, name, something about them. Collect info on each lead before sending.
- Write at below third-grade level.
- They don't trust us: Big Fast Value. Make the lead magnet exceptional, offer what others charge for, use $100M Offers. Give the biggest, fastest value you can afford.
- Scripts: phone or chat never over a page or two, cold email rarely over half a page. Tweak as you learn. 100 cold calls teach more than 100 books.

## How much
- Automate delivery: balance scale and personalization. Small audience: personalize to the highest degree. Use AI to find the balance.
- Automate distribution: manual (dial, click send) vs automated (1000 DMs or emails at once). Personalization depends on how important the lead is.
- Follow up more times in more ways: someone who never answers calls may answer DMs. Volume shows you are serious and creates fear of missing out.
- If someone asks not to be contacted, do not contact them. Spend time on neutrals, not negatives.
- After multiple contacts, wait 3 to 6 months and do it again. Volume negates luck: replicate, then iterate.
- Voicemail script: "Hi (first name), it's (your name). I'm calling in reference to (current customer's name). Please give me a call back at (number)." Works because it is short, it names a competitor not a company, and it opens a loop. Do not say "this is (first name)", do not mention your company, do not fail to follow up.
- Benchmark: decide how many leads to work per week. Too many and you waste them, no leads left behind.

## Daily checklist (Leo's table)
Who: yourself. What: lead magnet plus core offer. Where: any private platform. To whom: scraped, bought or software lists. When: every day, 7 days a week. How: live calls, voicemail drops, email and text blasts, DMs, video messages, direct mail. How much: 100 per day. How many times: 2x a day for the first few days, 1x after one week, then 1x a week. How long: as long as it takes.

## Why bother
No content or ads needed, competitors do not see it, very reliable, content works as nurture, fewer platform and regulation changes, compliance less painful, no spokesperson means a sellable business, hard to copy.

## Clinic constraints (Jarvis, check before acting)
- Mexico's privacy law (LFPDPPP) and WhatsApp rules apply to bought or scraped lists. Not verified for this plan.
- The voicemail script names another customer as a reference. Only use it with that customer's real permission, never invented.
