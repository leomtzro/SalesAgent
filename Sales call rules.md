Rules to follow before, during and after every sales conversation. Written as instructions for the sales agent (and for Leo).

## Before the call
1. Research the prospect for as long as the call will last (1-hour call = 1 hour of research).
2. Kill zombies upfront: ask, "Is there anyone else who needs to be on the call to make the decision?"
3. Read your notes from the last conversation. Notes are prep for the next call.
4. Have your key stories and metaphors ready for the expected objections ([[Objection drills]]).

## During the call
5. Only three things come out of your mouth: **questions, restatements, and short stories** (stories are used to break beliefs).
6. Listen twice as much as you talk. Intervene only when necessary, and make interventions surgical, not waffle. The prospect must not feel sold to; they must feel they bought.
7. Ask questions until they come up with the solution themselves. They need to convince themselves.
8. Answer questions with questions to align them. Ask about their past: what they liked and didn't like.
   - "What makes you better than the competition?" → "What are you looking for?"
   - "Tell me why you're different." → "What have you done in the past?"
9. Bring the script and know it like the bible, but don't memorize and wait to talk; if you do, you won't listen. Be concise. Never expand the script.
10. Ask hard questions and make them kill the zombies in front of you. Example: "My boss won't let me." → "Perfect, let's talk about reasons he would. What are you afraid of happening? Let's play it out: worst case X, best case Y."
11. Use childlike curiosity on objections: tilt your head, ask the question a child would ask, worded properly, and finish on a high note.
12. For contradictory objections, use humor first (prepare the lines in advance). Once the humor shows the objection doesn't make sense, switch to a serious tone: "What's really going on? What's the real concern driving all these objections?"
13. Seek to understand, not to argue: "I wouldn't have thought about that."
14. Book a meeting from a meeting (BAMFAM): never end without a date for the next conversation.

## Asking for the sale again
Only in this sequence:
1. Ask for the sale.
2. They say no.
3. Ask why.
4. They give the reason.
5. Resolve that reason.
6. Only now ask for the sale again.

## When they say yes early
If the prospect has already said yes, stop talking and close. Do not keep selling (Hoffman: the salesman with "18 more slides to go" got sent to wait in the car). See [[Second-slide customers]].

## After the call
15. Record every call, especially on a hot streak, and review it ([[Daily sales training]]).
16. Ask for referrals: "Who do you know that's as awesome as you and would be interested in this?"
17. If they bought: reinforce the decision within 48 hours (personal video or voice memo, handwritten card). See [[C.L.O.S.E.R]].
18. Log the outcome and the objections in the CRM.
