**Definition:** the share of people with an appointment who actually show up.

**Formula:** shows ÷ booked appointments.

**Benchmark:** 70% is good enough that show rate stops being a problem. Set the bar high for both appointments and show rate.

## What affects it
1. Number of time slots available.
2. Targeting and marketing (optimize for cheapest cost per sale, not cheapest lead).
3. Multi-tasking and contact habits:
   - Always double-dial.
   - Slow time-to-contact hurts it.
   - Appointment reminders always matter.

## How to fix it
For the discovery call:
- No aggressive sales.
- Shorten the time between calls: same day or next day.
- Send personalized reminders.

For the close call:
- Daily training: role-play and tone ([[Daily sales training]]).
- Game-tape (call recording) reviews.
- End-of-day reports.
