Takeaway: what the Chief Sales Officer does to carry out the [[Lead generation plan]]: build the list, contact it every day, qualify, hold the review and the call, and count everything. Changes to the plan are made in Strategy first.

## Orders from Strategy
1. **Build the list:** collect Tijuana clinics from Google Maps, test a small sample first, and count them. The count answers how many contacts a day are realistic ([[Clinic outreach by Google Maps and WhatsApp]], [[TAM and cold email volume]]).
2. **Warm first:** contact people we know and ask for introductions to clinic owners. Use the A.C.A. steps and the friend-invite message ([[Warm outreach]]). Aim: the first 5 founding clinics.
3. **Then cold:** personalize each message (name, something about their clinic), keep it under half a page, plain words ([[Cold outreach]]). Write scripts in Spanish.
4. **Daily checklist** (from [[Cold outreach]]): who yourself, what the review plus core offer, to whom the list, every day, several ways (call, WhatsApp, DM, voicemail), proposed 100 a day, follow up twice a day the first days then once a day, then weekly.
5. **Qualify before spending time:** score each clinic with the [[Customer scoresheet]] and talk to the best first.
6. **Hold the review and the call:** [[Discovery call]], [[The diagnostic sale]], [[C.L.O.S.E.R]], [[Sales call rules]].
7. **Count everything:** contacts, replies, reviews booked, calls, closes ([[Sales funnel metrics]], [[Close rate]], [[Show rate]]). Report weekly to Strategy.

## Rules
- Respect any "do not contact me".
- Before any bought or scraped list is used: the legal check in the Strategy plan must be done.
- The voicemail "in reference to a customer" only with a real customer's permission.
- The patient agent never reveals it is an AI; a sincere "are you a bot?" goes to a human.
- Reply promise: within 5 minutes in business hours until the full messaging pipeline exists.

## Open
First-clinic questions: is the first doctor call free and how long, who confirms bookings, who receives handoffs, who answers messages. See [[Open questions - Hormozi notes]].
