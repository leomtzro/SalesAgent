Practice for the [[Conviction framework]]: words are 10%, delivery is 90%.

## "I didn't say he hit his wife" exercise
Say the sentence "I didn't say he hit his wife" seven times, stressing a different word each time. Each stress changes the meaning. Then run the same tone analysis on every objection response and question in the script: which word carries the meaning, and what tone does it need?

## Tone rules
- A lower tone makes people listen and treat what you say as important.
- Use humor on contradictory objections, then switch to seriousness ([[Sales call rules]], rule 12).
- Ask objection questions with childlike curiosity, finishing on a high note.

Part of [[Daily sales training]] (25 minutes in the alternative split).
