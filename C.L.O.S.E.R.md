How to ask questions that make the prospect say yes. The first of Hormozi's three frameworks for simple, scalable sales, alongside the [[Conviction framework]] and the [[Daily sales training]] schedule. Simplicity is what scales.

## C: Clarify why they are on the call
1. Why are you here?
2. What made you come today?
3. What made you reach out?
4. What's your goal right now?
5. Why is that important to you?

## L: Label them with a problem
"It sounds like X is your goal, does that sound about right?"

## O: Overview of past pain
6. Tell me what you've done so far about this problem.
7. How did that work out for you?
8. How long did you do it for?
9. How long ago?
10. What else have you tried?

## S: Sell them (sell the vacation)
"This is what I'm getting from you, and this is how we'll solve your problem."
11. Would you like to hear how the program works?
12. There are X things that make clients successful.
13. Do not get technical (modules, systems). Anything that sounds like work, scrape it off.

## E: Explain away their concerns
Ask: "Do you want it?"
- "Yes" → Awesome.
- "No" → "Why?" → "XYZ" → solve XYZ, then ask if they want it again. See [[Asking for the sale again]].

What the concern questions sound like:
- Price → reframe to value (Ferrari). Open question #1 in [[Open questions - Hormozi notes]].
- Decision-maker → refer to their past agreements.
- Stall → confront their decision criteria. "Do they want to suffer forever?"
- What's your main concern?
- Do you feel the product can meet your needs?
- Do you want to work with us?
- Do you have access to funds, or know someone who does?

Full objection responses: [[The 5 excuses]].

## R: Reinforce the decision once they say yes
- A personalized video or voice memo.
- A handwritten card.
- If you sell something expensive, people decide how they feel about you within 48 hours.
