Booking is an ability problem when patients must call, fill a form or switch apps. Shrink it: book inside the same WhatsApp chat and offer two choices ("¿Martes 10:00 o miércoles 16:00?") instead of an open "¿cuándo quiere venir?". Follow with a one-tap confirmation and a reminder 24 hours before.

What to measure: booked appointments ÷ conversations answered, and [[Show rate]]. Our baseline is `unknown`; the 70% show-rate benchmark is from Hormozi (see [[Show rate]]), not from Fogg. Add "Our data" with dates once we have it.

Framework: [[Fogg behavior model]].
