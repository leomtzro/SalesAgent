Takeaway: the first clinics do not arrive on their own. We go and get them one by one, and we do the setup with them on the spot.

## Graham's pattern
- Stripe: when a company said it would try the product, the founders installed it right then ("the Collison installation") instead of sending a link. The weak version is "Will you try our beta?" followed by "we'll send you a link."
- Airbnb: founders went door to door in New York, recruited hosts, and helped them improve their listings.
- Founders skip this because of shyness (cold outreach means rejection), laziness, and because the early numbers look too small. The founder, usually the CEO, has to do the selling.
- Find the most enthusiastic early users and look for more of them in the same place. Pinterest found design bloggers and recruited at their conference.

## Steps for us
1. Start with the warm list: Leo's dad's introduction to the dermatologist, then Arturo, then the sister's medical-school network ([[2026-11-09 Office hours - Client zero before scale]], [[Warm outreach]]).
2. At the first meeting do not pitch: ask the five questions in the office-hours note and write down her exact words ([[Mom Test customer conversations]]).
3. If she agrees to the pilot, set it up in the same meeting: pick the baseline numbers, agree the 30 days, take the first access step together. Not "I'll send you a link later."
4. End every call with a next step that costs her something small: [[Commitment ladder]].
5. After each clinic, note which type of owner was most enthusiastic and go find more like them: [[Start in one narrow niche]].
6. Only after about 20 conversations decide the niche ([[Lead generation plan]]).

## Choose speed over size at first
Graham: if you must choose between the users who sign up fastest and the users who would pay most, take the fastest; they are the early adopters. For us: the owner who says yes to a measured free pilot this week beats the larger clinic that says "later."

## Example line (draft, Spanish to be reviewed)
"Si quiere, lo configuramos ahora mismo juntos en 20 minutos y mañana ya está recibiendo respuestas." English: "If you want, we set it up together right now in 20 minutes and tomorrow it is already answering."

## Limits
Graham says manual recruiting is hard where you have no connections. Our connections beyond the first three are `unknown`. Cold WhatsApp rules for clinics are still unchecked ([[Lead generation plan]], decision 4).
