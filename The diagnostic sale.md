Selling like a doctor: diagnose first, then prescribe, then present the price in a set order.

## Before the call: pre-sale questionnaire
- Works like an application form.
- Collects card details and completes the customer profile: "It's just how the system works."

## The diagnosis
1. **Current state:** where they are.
2. **Desired state:** where they want to be.
3. **Obstacle:** what's in the way. When they name it, explain their obstacle back to them.

## The prescription
- Present the desired state and the vehicle that overcomes the obstacle: exactly what they want and how it will happen.
- Let them set how successful they want to be, and so how much they're willing to pay.
- Give them an incentive to prepay.
- If you upsell, the upsell price must be at least 5x the normal price.

## Presenting the price (transactional or recurring)
Always present the full price first, then the prepayment discount, then the half option.

**Step-downs, in order:**
1. Paid in full (card or third-party financing).
2. Half discount. (Open question #2 in [[Open questions - Hormozi notes]].)
3. Continuity: build a financing plan with them on the spot.
4. Rebooking: "Let me know when you want this product or service."
