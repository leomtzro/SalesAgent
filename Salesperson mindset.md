The attitude to hold every day. The beliefs behind it are in [[Sales beliefs]].

## Be a tiger
Three types of salespeople:
1. **Dogs:** you have to feed them; if you don't, they starve.
2. **Horses:** they gallop only while you keep whipping them.
3. **Tigers:** they kill for sport, even on a full belly.

Be the tiger. Treat every unqualified lead as what it is: free practice with virtually no downside and some upside. Practicing with the worst leads is what makes you a tiger.

## Rules
- No is the job. Expect it and plan for it.
- Don't take rejection personally: great salespeople collect more nos than mediocre ones.
- See everything as a skill: be consistent and sustain a high level of enthusiasm.
- Never blame circumstances; focus on what you control.
- Do it consistently for a very long time ([[The 3 jobs of a salesperson]]).
