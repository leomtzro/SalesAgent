Takeaway: say plainly, on the discovery call and on the website, who our service is not for. Prospects tend to trust a seller who turns down poor fits, and we protect our time from the clients who cost the most.

## Method
1. Write three disqualifiers from experience. Draft examples: clinics that do not reply to patients the same day; clinics that want no process change; owners who negotiate every line.
2. State them early in a calm tone: "No es para clínicas que no responden a sus pacientes en el mismo día ni quieren cambiar su proceso."
3. When a prospect fails one, say so kindly and offer a later date or a smaller step. Do not discount to win them.
4. Record why each was turned away in [[Customer scoresheet]] so the criteria improve.

## Why it holds elsewhere (shared rule)
Exclusion is part of the product's signal. Elite schools, restaurants with a dress code and specialist surgeons gain value from who they refuse.
