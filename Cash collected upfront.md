**Definition:** cash collected at the sale compared with the total value sold.

**Formula:** cash collected upfront ÷ total contract value sold.

**What it tells you:** buyer incentives. If people only buy the cheapest or most spread-out option, ask whether they're buying because it's cheap.

**Benchmark:** unknown (open question #11 in [[Open questions - Hormozi notes]]).

**How to raise it:** present the full price first, then the prepayment discount, then the step-downs. See [[The diagnostic sale]].

Part of [[Sales funnel metrics]].
