Takeaway: in any kickoff, call or onboarding meeting where we need the honest view of a junior person, ask everyone to write their answer alone first, then read the answers out one at a time.

## Method
1. Ask one question on paper or in chat ("¿Qué podría hacer que esto no funcione?").
2. Give 3 to 5 minutes of silent writing.
3. Go around the room, one answer each. The most junior person goes first; the owner and Leo go last.
4. Write the answers down in front of everyone and say who will act on which.

## Clinic example
In an onboarding kickoff the owner says the process is fine. The receptionist writes: "Los pacientes nuevos llegan por WhatsApp y yo no sé cuál responder primero." That is the handover rule to build. Said aloud after the owner, she would likely have kept quiet.

## Why it holds elsewhere (shared rule)
Independent judgments carry more information than judgments that influence each other. Jury deliberation, hiring panels and wine tastings score privately before discussing.

Used inside [[Premortem for clinic pilots]]. Pair with [[Mom Test customer conversations]] when the question is about the past, not a forecast.

## Limits
Proposed. Whether a Tijuana receptionist answers frankly in writing with the owner in the room: `unknown`. Anonymous slips of paper are an alternative if not.
