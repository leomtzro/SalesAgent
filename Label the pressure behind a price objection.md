When a clinic owner says "Es muy caro", do not defend the price. Mirror ("¿Muy caro?"), stay silent, then label: "Suena a que lo difícil es justificar el gasto con los socios." Listen, then offer the pilot or a per-booked-appointment component.

Patient-facing agent: label first ("Entiendo que el costo es una preocupación importante"), then give only the approved price range and a payment plan if the clinic allows it. No medical claims.

Drill it with [[Objection drills]] and [[Tonality drills]]. Method: [[Label mirror silence objection method]]. Spanish phrasing is adapted and untested.
