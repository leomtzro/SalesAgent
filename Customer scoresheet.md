A 100-point profile that scores how likely a prospect is to be a fan ([[Second-slide customers]]).

## Who tends to buy
People who have tried the same thing for about 5 years and want to make a name for themselves.

## Adjustments in the notes
| Signal | Points |
|---|---|
| New in their job (no pain yet) | -20 |
| Coming up on their 5th anniversary | +15 |
| Budget authority is below the price and they need approval for more funds | -30 |
| Never used a competitor | -5 |
| Used a competitor, great experience | -40 |
| Used a competitor, bad experience | +30 |

## How to use it
- Only talk to people in the 90s. Talk to people in the 80s only in an extreme situation.
- In discovery, tell them you'd like to get to know them before the closing call. See [[Discovery call]].

## Unknown
The starting score is not in the notes, and the 5-year mark is assumed to count time in their job (Leo confirmed "new" means new in the job; the anniversary is not confirmed). See [[Open questions - Hoffman notes]]. These weights come from the lecture, not from clinics, so treat them as an example until we have our own.
