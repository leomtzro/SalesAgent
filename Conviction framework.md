How anyone can outperform a seasoned sales rep by learning to control their tone. The second of Hormozi's three frameworks, with [[C.L.O.S.E.R]] and [[Daily sales training]].

- Words are 10%; 90% is how you say them.
- A lower tone makes people listen, because they assume what you say is important.
- Conviction corrects your tone. Build conviction by:
  1. Re-reading your testimonials every day.
  2. Fixing everything you possibly can about the product. Never blame a customer for lack of success.
  3. Never stopping improving, so you know you put the work in.
