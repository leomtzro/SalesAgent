Takeaway: efficiency beats creativity. Find the ads with the most return and make a lot of those. Ad budget should remove customer acquisition as a constraint.

## How much to spend
Phase 1: track money. Phase 2: lose money (when you find a winner, double down). Phase 3: print money (squeeze the winner).

## How well you are doing
- Lifetime gross profit = (profit minus costs) x times bought.
- Return on ad spend: lifetime gross profit / cost of acquisition. Above 3 is a home run. You can raise LTGP but must fulfill the promise.
- Ethical monopoly: whoever spends the least to acquire a customer can outbid everyone, since most paid ads are auctions.

## If ads are not profitable
Upsell to clients (client-financed acquisition): upsell profit covers or exceeds acquisition cost, so you raise spend and go after more clients. Needs innovation. Easiest upsells: more, better, or new/different.

## What I wish I knew
1. Do not confuse sales problems with ad problems: if the right people are on the phone, it is a sales problem.
2. Your best free content can make your best ads.
3. If you say you suck at something, you will probably suck at it.
4. The first few ads are learning budget: "I expect to lose 1000 USD a month until I hit a home run."
