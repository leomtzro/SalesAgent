**Definition:** the share of people who buy. It can be measured two ways:
- **On shows:** buyers ÷ people who showed up.
- **On offers:** buyers ÷ people you made an offer to.
Always say which one you're reporting.

**Benchmark:** 40% is healthy.

**Referrals** close at 80–90%, so always ask for them.

## How to fix it
- Add a video sales letter.
- Role-play the common objections ([[Objection drills]]).
- Focus on the experience, not the features.
- Optimize the script ([[C.L.O.S.E.R]]).

Part of [[Sales funnel metrics]].
