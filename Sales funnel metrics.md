The map of the sales numbers to track, and the order to fix them in. Track the data so you can give the best salespeople the best leads.

## The chain
[[Show rate]] → [[Offer rate]] → [[Close rate]] → [[Cash collected upfront]] → **units sold**.

Units sold is the output of the other four.

## The funnel stages (two-call model)
1. People who schedule the discovery call.
2. People who show up to the discovery call.
3. People who schedule the close call.
4. People who show up to the close call.
5. People who buy.

## Benchmarks from the notes
| Metric | Benchmark |
|---|---|
| Show rate | 70% is good enough that show rate stops being a problem |
| Close rate | 40% is healthy |
| Referral close rate | 80–90% |
| Buyers you can't lose / can't win | about 10% each; fight for the middle 80% |
| Share of revenue going to the sales team | under 10% is a possible pain point (see [[Outsourced vs in-house revenue teams]]) |

## Lead cost rule
Your lead metric should be the cheapest **cost per sale**, not the cheapest lead.

## What to fix, in order
1. Founder acting as sales manager → hire a sales manager (focus on the who).
2. Low show rate → fix ad targeting.
3. Low show rate → reduce the sales team and reset expectations (underused time).
4. Low show rate → build a bridge between marketing and sales (a lead nurture specialist).
5. Low close rate → optimize the sales script.

Practice with the worst leads: see [[Salesperson mindset]].
