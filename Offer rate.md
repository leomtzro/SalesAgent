**Definition:** the share of people on calls that you actually make an offer to.

**Formula:** offers made ÷ calls held.

**What it tells you:** the quality of the lead flow. A low offer rate means many leads aren't qualified (no problem to solve or no money to spend).

**Benchmark:** unknown (open question #10 in [[Open questions - Hormozi notes]]).

**Common error:** over-educating on the product and under-educating yourself on the prospect. See [[Discovery call]].

**To raise the schedule rate for the close call:** stop qualifying too early, and qualify properly instead of just stating the price: make sure they have the money and the problem. Compensate setters and closers for it. The exact meaning of "stop early qualification" and the comp structure are open questions #8–9.

Part of [[Sales funnel metrics]].
