The first step of outbound is to build a **TAM**: a database of all your prospects (TAM = total addressable market, everyone you could sell to).

## Data to collect for each prospect
First name, last name, email, job title, and where they live or where their HQ is.

## Cold email volume
Estimated cold emails needed to grow the customer base: **2,000**. Why: "it's a numbers game."

## Unknown
2,000 per week, per month or in total, and how many clients that should produce, are not in the notes. See [[Open questions - Roque meeting]].
